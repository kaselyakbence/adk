# ADK Website

At its core, this is meant to be a simple website for the dorm - a place
to find out about the dorm, how to get in touch, and what's going on
here, the same way any dorm or building website would. Alongside that,
I've built one particularly important feature worth showing off first:
real-time laundry machine booking.

## Laundry machine booking

A simple website for the dorm that shows, in real time, which washing
machines and dryers are free right now. No more walking down to the
basement just to check, and no more guessing whether a running machine
belongs to someone or was just left there, done, hours ago.

## How it works

1. Open the site on your phone. No app to download - though you can add
   it to your home screen if you want it to feel like one.
2. See at a glance which of the 5 washing machines and 3 dryers are free
   right now, and how much time is left on the ones that are running.
3. When you're at a machine and ready to start it, scan the QR code taped
   to it with your phone's camera.
4. Enter how long you'll be washing or drying, then hit Start. The site
   now knows that machine is busy, and for how long.
5. Optionally, get a notification on your phone the moment your load is
   done - no need to keep walking down just to check.

That's the whole idea: replace "walk down and hope" with "check your
phone."

## What could come next

A few ideas for where the site could go after this, once it's up and
running:

- **Event calendar** - a page where anyone can add or edit upcoming dorm
  events (parties, quiz nights, movie nights), so everyone always knows
  what's coming up. Could even send a phone notification shortly before
  an event starts.
- **Report a problem** - a simple form to report something broken (a
  machine, a light, anything) without having to track someone down in
  person. Can be sent straight to the Housmeiser as an email.
- **Announcements board** - a place for the SSV to post notices (like
  "water will be off Thursday morning"), with the option to send it
  straight to people's phones instead of a paper sign nobody reads.
- **A shared dorm password** - one password, known only to people who
  live here, would be needed to actually start a machine or report a
  problem. Just looking at the site - checking which machines are free,
  reading about the dorm - would stay open to everyone, no password
  needed.
